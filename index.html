Comprehensive Specialities

Holistic Medical Care & Diagnostic Precision

From preventive cardiology and metabolic management to in-house ultrasonography and minor procedures.

Preventive Cardiology

Early detection of coronary risk, 12-lead digital ECG, 2D Echocardiography, ambulatory BP telemetry, and advanced lipidology.

  • 2D Echo with Color Doppler
  • ApoB & hs-CRP Cardiac Profiling
  • Hypertension Titration Protocol

Diabetes & Metabolic Health

Tailored glycemic control protocols, continuous glucose monitoring (CGM), diabetic neuropathy screening, and thyroid management.

  • HbA1c & Fasting Insulin Analysis
  • Diabetic Foot & Neuropathy Check
  • Reversal Diet & Lifestyle Coaching

In-House Diagnostics

NABL-calibrated fully automated biochemistry, hematology, hormone assays, and digital ultrasonography with same-day reports.

  • Whole Abdomen & Pelvic Ultrasound
  • Thyroid, Liver & Kidney Function Tests
  • Barcoded Digital Patient Reports

Family & Preventive Care

Continuous primary healthcare for all age groups, annual master health checkups, adult immunizations, and chronic disease supervision.

  • Annual Master Health Checkups
  • Adult & Travel Vaccinations
  • Geriatric & Arthritis Care

Women's Health & Hormones

Dedicated protocols for PCOS/PCOD, perimenopause, osteopenia screening, breast wellness examinations, and metabolic balance.

  • PCOS & Menstrual Irregularity Care
  • Thyroid & Vitamin D Profiling
  • Bone Mineral Density Evaluation

Minor Surgery & Day-Care

Sterile outpatient surgical suite for minor excisions, cyst enucleation, diabetic wound care, suturing, and aseptic dressings.

  • Sterile Sebaceous Cyst Removal
  • Advanced Hydrocolloid Wound Care
  • Ear Syringing & Foreign Body Triage

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your visit or diagnostic test.

For lipid profiles, fasting blood sugar, and whole abdomen ultrasound scans, 10 to 12 hours of overnight fasting (water is permitted) is strictly recommended. For routine blood counts (CBC), thyroid panels (TSH), and non-fasting sugar, no prior fasting is required.

Most routine biochemistry, hematology, and ECG reports are delivered within 3 to 4 hours on the same day. Patients receive a secure WhatsApp PDF copy and digital download link, with physical printed reports available at our reception.

Walk-ins are welcomed throughout our morning and evening hours. However, to eliminate waiting time and guarantee an unhurried consultation, we strongly advise booking a preferred slot through our online portal or front desk helpline.
